[CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION & SECURE ADMIN SIGN-IN]

Welcome to the official video training guide for the Lanka News Hub newsroom
workspace. In this walkthrough, we will demonstrate how to easily manage and
publish content on your news portal.

Whether you are publishing a fast-breaking story, adding high-resolution
photography, scheduling news releases, managing sponsored advertisements,
or onboarding new journalists, this dashboard gives you total control.

To get started, open your web browser and go to your website address followed
by /admin/login, or simply click 'Member Account' in the top navigation bar.
Enter your administrator email and password. If you want to check what you’ve
typed, click the eye icon to toggle visibility, then press Sign In.


[CHAPTER 2: THE NEWSROOM DASHBOARD OVERVIEW]

Once you log in, you are greeted by the Newsroom Overview. This is your mission
control center.

At the very top, you’ll see live metric cards showing:
1. Total Published Stories,
2. Drafts awaiting editorial review,
3. Total Story Views across all readers, and
4. Media library items.

Directly below these stats is your Recent Stories table. Here you can see your
newest articles at a glance, monitor how many views each story is receiving,
check publication status, and click straight into any story to edit it.

In the upper-right corner, you’ll find the 'Visit website' shortcut, which lets
you open the public front page in a separate tab to immediately see how your live
site looks to readers.


[CHAPTER 3: WRITING & PUBLISHING A NEWS STORY]

Now let's publish a news story.

In the top right or the left sidebar, click the red '+ Write a story' button.
This opens the multi-edition story editor.

One of Lanka News Hub's strongest capabilities is full trilingual publishing.
Notice the three language tabs at the top: English, Sinhala, and Tamil. You can
publish in a single language, or provide translations across all three editions
so readers in any language edition enjoy a native experience.

When you enter your article headline, the system automatically builds an
SEO-friendly URL slug.

Next, add your summary excerpt and paste or type your article body in the content area.

On the right side of the editor, you will configure essential story metadata:
- News Category: Select the appropriate desk, such as Local, Politics, Business,
  Sports, Entertainment, or Technology.
- Breaking News Checkbox: Checking this immediately sends the story to the
  prominent red 24x7 scrolling ticker at the very top of the site.
- Featured Story: Select this if you want the article to take prime placement
  as the main broadsheet hero on the front page.
- Featured Image: Click 'Select Media' to choose an eye-catching photo from your
  media shelf or upload a new photo directly.

Before saving, select your Publication Status:
- Draft: Keeps the article hidden from readers while you write and refine it.
- Published: Makes the article instantly live across the front page, category
  pages, and search feeds.
- Scheduled: Allows you to pick a future date and time in Sri Lanka time. The
  platform's automated background runner will publish the story at that exact
  moment without anyone needing to be at their computer.
- Archived: Unpublishes older stories from the public listing while safely keeping
  their content and analytics in your records.

When you're finished, click Save story. A confirmation message will appear,
and your story is published.


[CHAPTER 4: MANAGING THE MEDIA LIBRARY]

Next, let’s look at the Media Library in the sidebar.

This is your central vault for all photography, event banners, infographics,
and press releases.

To add media, simply click Upload files and select images from your computer.
Our backend media processor automatically compresses your photos and converts
them into next-generation WebP format. This drastically reduces file size while
keeping high visual clarity, ensuring rapid page loads for readers on mobile
networks across Sri Lanka.

Every image card features a Copy URL button, making it effortless to link images
inside articles. The platform also has built-in safety controls: it will notify
you and block accidental deletion of any image currently featured in an active
story or advertisement.


[CHAPTER 5: NEWS CATEGORIES & DESKS]

Clicking on Categories brings up the configuration for your main editorial
desks—such as Business Wire, Sports Central, Tech Desk, and Life & Culture.

Here, you can:
- Add new categories as your coverage expands,
- Reorder categories to adjust their sequence in the main navigation bar, and
- Input Sinhala and Tamil names for each category to ensure native typographic
  accuracy across all language editions.


[CHAPTER 6: AD PUBLISHING & DIGITAL MONETIZATION]

Now, let's explore Ad Publishing, which powers your publication's revenue.

Lanka News Hub comes pre-configured with 11 high-visibility ad placements—including
the header leaderboard, homepage top banner, in-article mid-content slots,
sidebar blocks, and the persistent bottom floating bar.

When creating an ad campaign:
1. Give your campaign a descriptive title.
2. Choose the target placement from the dropdown.
3. Select the format: Display Image Banner, Text Sponsorship, or Custom HTML / Script.
4. Enter your sponsor's destination link and upload the banner asset.

If you have multiple advertisers booked for the same slot, the system automatically
loops through them in a smooth rotation, giving every sponsor fair exposure.

You can also click 'Ad Placement Preview' to see all 11 ad slots highlighted with
outline guides across the live website, making it simple to demonstrate placement
value to potential corporate advertisers.


[CHAPTER 7: SITE SETTINGS, HOTLINE & GOOGLE ADSENSE]

Under Site & languages, administrators can configure overall website parameters:
- Publication Name: Update the brand name displayed across the browser tab and search engines.
- Public Contact Email & Hotline: Update the hotline phone number (e.g. 011 5 300 800),
  which updates automatically in the top bar, footer, and support cards.
- Language Edition Toggles: Enable or disable English, Sinhala, or Tamil editions with
  a single checkbox.
- Google AdSense Configuration: If your publication uses AdSense, simply enter your
  Publisher Client ID here. The system handles all tag insertion safely across desktop
  and mobile without requiring any code edits.


[CHAPTER 8: PEOPLE, ROLES & SECURITY LOGS]

Under People & roles, you can invite journalists, editors, and administrators.

There are distinct user roles:
- Reporters: Can write and manage their own drafts.
- Editors: Can review, edit, and publish stories across all desks.
- Super Administrators: Have complete administrative access, including site settings
  and staff management.

Finally, the Activity log keeps a clean, time-stamped record of all newsroom activity—
such as sign-ins, story publications, status changes, and settings updates—giving your
leadership complete operational transparency.


[CHAPTER 9: SUMMARY & CLOSING]

And that completes the training walkthrough for the Lanka News Hub administrative
dashboard.

With these tools at your fingertips, your newsroom can deliver rapid, high-impact
journalism to readers across Sri Lanka and worldwide.

If you ever need further assistance, consult your accompanying written reference
guide or contact our technical support desk. Thank you, and happy publishing!
